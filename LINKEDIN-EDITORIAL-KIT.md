## Carousel C — What professional QA taught me about automation

1. **Automation is a delivery problem.** Source: Condor Gaming QA/project experience.
2. **Define what done means.** Acceptance criteria, dependencies and handoffs.
3. **Test the unhappy path.** Missing fields, duplicates, integration failures, unclear ownership.
4. **Make decisions visible.** Logs, escalation, documentation and release gates.
5. **Transfer across industries.** From digital teams to logistics to independent systems work: reliability depends on clarity.

Draft caption:
> My experience across project coordination, QA, customer operations and logistics taught me something simple: a process is only reliable when the team knows what should happen when it doesn't go to plan.
>
> I now apply that thinking to practical automation and connected digital tools. The goal isn't to replace judgment. It's to reduce guesswork and make handoffs clearer.
